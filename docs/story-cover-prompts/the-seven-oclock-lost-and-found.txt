Create a distinctive illustrated cover for an adult Chinese reading story about a railway station lost-property office, a misplaced bag, and the last recording of a beloved wife. The visual direction is a sophisticated paper-cut editorial illustration: sculptural layers of colored paper, subtly fibrous surfaces, crisp silhouettes, gentle shallow shadows, and beautifully restrained human expressions. Make it emotionally warm, quietly suspenseful, and immediately legible as a small library thumbnail.

Use a horizontal 3:2 composition designed to crop cleanly to 16:9. Keep the essential subjects within the central seventy percent of the height. On the counter in the central foreground sits a worn indigo-blue cloth bag. Its repaired handle is tied with a thick mustard-yellow cord, visibly finished with two knots. A small, genuinely old portable cassette recorder peeks from the open bag; its buttons and speaker grille are simple, convincing shapes. These are the unmistakable focal objects. Beside the bag, the hand of an elderly Chinese man rests gently on the counter. Show his silver-haired face in three-quarter view, dignified, tired, and beginning to feel hopeful.

In the quieter background, suggest a modest Chinese railway station office with a few storage lockers, one moving carton, a platform window, and an analog wall clock whose hands are nearing seven. Render the clock without numerals. Warm evening light touches the yellow cord and the man's hand, while slate-blue station shadows frame the scene. Use generous uncluttered shapes and a strong blue, ochre, cream, and muted coral palette. The mystery concerns care and mistaken belongings, not violence or fear. No title, lettering, readable labels, logos, watermark, ghosts, weapons, police tape, or theatrical horror.

Create a tender, quietly cinematic illustrated cover for an adult contemporary romance set during the final evening shift at a neighborhood Chinese bookshop. The bookseller is leaving for a job in another city tomorrow; a familiar customer has returned a book containing their shared thoughts, and rain gives them time to speak honestly. Choose the visual language of an original hand-painted animated film: expressive but understated adult characters, delicate pencil contours, luminous gouache backgrounds, softened watercolor edges, and a little visible paper grain. It should feel intimate and lived in, never childish or like a glossy dating advertisement.

Compose a horizontal 3:2 image that can be cropped centrally to 16:9. Preserve both faces, the book, and the map within the central seventy percent of the height. Frame the scene from slightly outside a rain-streaked bookshop window, with just a few translucent droplets at the edges rather than obscuring the subjects. Inside, two Chinese adults around thirty stand on opposite sides of a small wooden reading table. One is a casually dressed man with a rain-darkened jacket; the bookseller has an understated, androgynous appearance. They exchange a small, attentive smile across a comfortable space. Their poses convey mutual interest and respect, without an embrace or a forced romantic climax.

On the table, a well-loved open book holds a loose note with tiny abstract annotation marks. A folded old city map shows a winding river without readable labels. A small amber reading lamp illuminates these objects. Suggest shelves, two cups, and a key near the counter with economical detail. Contrast warm honey and faded rose inside against blue-gray rain and muted green reflections outside.
